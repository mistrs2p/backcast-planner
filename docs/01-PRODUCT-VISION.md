# Product Vision

## Vision
Help a user start from a desired future state and maintain a feasible, measurable, adaptive path to it.

## MVP Promise
“Define where you want to be; the system works backwards to determine what must happen and what you can do now, then adapts the plan as reality changes.”

## MVP Scope
Goal, Future State, Backcasting, Milestones, Capacity, Calendar, Tasks, Progress, Feedback, Replanning.

## Out of Scope for MVP
Social features, gamification, mobile-native app, advanced ML optimization, complex multi-goal optimization, multi-future scenarios.
