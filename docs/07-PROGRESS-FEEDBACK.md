# Progress & Feedback

## Data Separation
Planned ≠ Actual ≠ Progress.

## Measurements
Support count, duration, percentage, boolean, score and future composite metrics.

## Variance
Interpret according to metric direction: maximize, minimize, target.

## Signals
Progress variance, time variance, capacity variance, schedule variance, trend, sustainability, completion rate, on-time rate.

## Feedback
Explicit user statements and implicit behavioral signals.
